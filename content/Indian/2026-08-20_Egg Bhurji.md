1. chop
   1. half onion
   2. half capsicum
2. in wok on medium heat (6), fry onion
3. when onion browning, add capsicum
4. when capsicum a bit softer add
   1. 1/2 spoon chilli powder
   2. 1/2 spoon haldi
   3. 1 spoon salt
   4. handful of crushed coriander
5. mix
6. crack 4 eggs into the wok, and immediately start mixing
7. cook for a few minutes until dry enough
